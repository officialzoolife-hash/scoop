Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with Lucky Scoop! We strive to deliver your cute aesthetic goodies and mystery scoops as quickly and safely as possible.

1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-4 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays.

2. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping charges for your orders will be calculated and displayed at checkout. Standard delivery typically takes 3-5 business days across Pakistan.

3. Shipment Confirmation & Order Tracking

You will receive a shipment confirmation email once your order has shipped containing your tracking number(s).
